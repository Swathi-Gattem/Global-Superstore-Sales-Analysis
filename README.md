# Global-Superstore-Sales-Analysis
Interactive Power BI dashboard built using the Global Superstore 2016 dataset to analyze sales, profit, customers, regions, and product performance. Features KPI tracking, trend analysis, customer segmentation, and business insights through dynamic visualizations and interactive filters for data-driven decision making.

# 📊 Project Title:
**Global Superstore 2016 Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard**

# 📝 Project Description:
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales, profit, customer behavior, regional performance, and product trends using the Global Superstore 2016 dataset. The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights, enabling users to track KPIs, identify top-performing categories, and support data-driven decision-making.

# 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used:

* 📈 **Power BI** – Dashboard Development & Visualization
* 🧮 **DAX** – KPI Calculations & Measures
* 🔄 **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation
* 📊 **Microsoft Excel** – Data Source
* 📉 **Data Visualization Techniques** – Charts, KPIs, Slicers & Treemaps

# 📂 Dataset Source:

* 🌐 **Dataset:** Global Superstore 2016
* 📋 **Type:** Retail Sales Dataset
* 🏢 **Domain:** Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics
* 📌 Contains information related to sales, profit, customers, orders, regions, categories, shipping modes, and market performance across multiple regions.

# ✨ Features & Highlights:

* 💰 Tracked **Total Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, Orders, and Customers**
* 📅 Analyzed **Monthly Sales Trends** and business growth patterns
* 🌍 Evaluated **Regional Sales & Profit Performance**
* 🛒 Compared **Sales Across Product Categories and Sub-Categories**
* 🚚 Examined **Shipping Mode Distribution and Impact**
* 👥 Performed **Customer Segmentation Analysis**
* 🎯 Identified **Top Customers and High-Performing Products**
* 🔍 Enabled **Interactive Filtering** using slicers for dynamic analysis
* 📌 Generated **Actionable Business Insights** to support strategic decision-making

🚀 **Outcome:** Delivered a user-friendly Business Intelligence dashboard that helps stakeholders monitor performance, discover trends, and make informed business decisions through interactive data visualization.
